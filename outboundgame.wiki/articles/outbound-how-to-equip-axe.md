---
title: How to Equip the Axe in Outbound
date: 2026-05-12
category: Guides
excerpt: A quick guide to placing your axe on the hotbar and switching to it for chopping.
---

**Prerequisite:** The Axe has already been crafted at your van’s workbench.

1. Press `Tab` to open your backpack.
2. In the **“Items”** tab, find the Axe.
3. Drag the Axe with your mouse down to the hotbar (any slot 1‑9).
4. Release – the axe now appears in that hotbar slot.
5. Press the number key that matches the slot to equip the axe.
6. ⚠️ The axe cannot be placed in the clothing/equipment slot; it must stay on the hotbar.
