🧭 When I first played Outbound, I wandered aimlessly for hours before realising I'd done everything in the wrong order. This route will save you from the same frustration — follow it and you'll have a rock‑solid foundation in under two hours.

---

## 🗺️ Step 1: Gather Litter (0–10 min)

You spawn beside an orange camper van with nothing but the clothes on your back.

- 🔍 **Collect Litter** — white bags scattered around the van.
- 📦 Aim for **10 pieces**.
- ♻️ Feed them into the **Recycler** at the back of your van to craft **5 Download Vouchers**.

---

## 🗼 Step 2: Unlock the First Signal Tower (10–20 min)

- 🚶 Head **southeast** toward the Fire Lookout tower.
- 💻 Interact with the computer beneath it.
- 🪓 Spend **1 Voucher** to download the **Axe** blueprint. Ignore everything else for now.

---

## 🪵 Step 3: Craft an Axe and Chop Trees (20–40 min)

- 🔨 Return to your van and craft the Axe at the Workbench (requires 2 Small Wood + 1 Stone).
- 🌳 Chop **10 regular trees** → ~20 Regular Wood Logs.
- 🪓 Chop **5 dead trees** → ~10 Everwood Logs.

---

## 🏭 Step 4: Unlock the Sawmill (40–50 min)

- 🔙 Go back to the Fire Lookout computer.
- 🪚 Spend **1 Voucher** on the **Sawmill** blueprint.
- 🏗️ Build the Sawmill inside your van.
- 🔄 Process your logs into planks:
  - **2 Regular Logs = 1 Plank**
  - **2 Everwood Logs = 1 Everwood Plank**

---

## 🌉 Step 5: Repair the Northern Bridge (50–70 min)

- 🧱 You need **10 Everwood Planks**.
- 🚶 Walk to the broken bridge north of your starting area.
- 🔧 Press `E` to repair — the bridge lowers, unlocking the Coast.

---

## 🏖️ Step 6: Enter the Coast (70–120 min)

- 🚐 Drive into the new region.
- 🐶 Visit **Paws &amp; Whiskers Lodge** to adopt a pet dog.
- 🗼 Unlock nearby Signal Towers for advanced blueprints.
- 🔍 Explore and gather resources at your own pace.

---

## ❌ Common Newbie Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts |
|---------|--------------|
| Exploring too far without tools | You'll die or waste time backtracking |
| Spending vouchers on non‑essential blueprints | Axe and Sawmill must come first |
| Burning wood as fuel | Wood is for planks — use fiber for fuel instead |
| Forgetting to save | Walk into your van before any big task |

> ⏱️ Follow this route and you'll hit the Coast in under two hours, fully equipped and ready for mid‑game. No wasted steps, no regrets.
