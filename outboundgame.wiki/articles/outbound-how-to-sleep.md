😴 One of the most common questions from new players is: *"Where is the sleep button?"* Outbound's sleep system isn't a button you can press anytime — it's tied closely to the day‑night cycle. I was stuck on this too, until I figured out how it works. Here's the complete guide.

---

## 🌙 When Can You Sleep?

Outbound has a built‑in day‑night rhythm: **day lasts about 20 minutes**, and **night lasts about 10 minutes**. The sleep option only unlocks once evening arrives — roughly **after 6 PM in‑game**.

During daylight hours, your bed will show: *"It's not time to sleep yet."* This design intentionally pushes you to explore and gather resources during the day, then rest at night.

---

## 🛏️ How to Sleep (Step by Step)

When evening arrives, follow these steps:

1. 🚐 Return to your orange camper van.
2. 🚪 Open the back door and walk inside.
3. 🛌 Approach the bed until the white **"Sleep"** prompt appears.
4. ⌨️ Press `E` to start sleeping.
5. 🎬 A short cutscene plays, and you wake up at **6:00 AM** the next morning.

---

## ✨ Benefits of Sleeping

Sleeping isn't just about skipping night — it provides crucial recovery benefits:

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| ❤️ **Full health restoration** | No matter how much damage you took, you wake up at full HP |
| ⚡ **Full stamina recovery** | Your energy bar is completely refilled |
| 🔄 **World reset** | All resources, creatures, and chests respawn |
| ⚠️ **Hunger penalty** | Sleeping consumes hunger — always eat after waking up |

---

## 🧠 Why You Should Sleep Every Night

Many newcomers try to power through the night. I strongly recommend against it:

- 🌑 **Night is dangerous**: More creatures spawn, and visibility is terrible.
- 🌿 **Resources refresh**: Sleep resets all gathering nodes — you'll have fresh materials at dawn.
- 💪 **Peak efficiency**: Starting the day with full health and stamina is a massive advantage.

## ❓ Common Questions

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Can I sleep outside my van? | ❌ No — only the bed inside your camper van works |
| Does sleeping save the game? | ✅ Yes — the game auto‑saves when you sleep |
| What if I don't sleep? | You can stay awake, but you'll face more danger and lower productivity |
| How does sleep work in multiplayer? | 🛌 If **one player** sleeps, **everyone** skips to morning together |

> 🌅 **Pro tip:** If you're caught far from your van at nightfall, find a safe hiding spot and wait it out — or log off and rejoin during daylight.
