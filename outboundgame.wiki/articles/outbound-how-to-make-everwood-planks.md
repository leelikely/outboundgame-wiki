---
title: How to Make Everwood Planks in Outbound
date: 2026-05-12
category: Guides
excerpt: Transform Everwood Logs into Everwood Planks using the Sawmill – essential for advanced building and bridge repairs.
---

**Prerequisites:** Sawmill blueprint unlocked and Sawmill built inside your van.

1. Obtain **Everwood Logs** by chopping dead trees or collecting them from riverside forests.
2. Go to your van and stand beside the Sawmill.
3. Press `E` to open the crafting interface.
4. Drag Everwood Logs from your backpack into the left input slot.
5. Select **“Everwood Plank”** from the recipe list on the right.
6. Click **“Craft”** to start processing.
7. 📐 Conversion ratio: **2 Everwood Logs → 1 Everwood Plank**.
