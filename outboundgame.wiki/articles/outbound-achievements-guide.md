# Outbound All Achievements Guide

Unlock every achievement across all platforms. This guide covers 32 achievements (worth 1000 points) including the 8 hidden ones.

---

## 🎯 Story‑Related (6 Achievements)

| Achievement | Points | How to unlock |
|-------------|--------|---------------|
| Journey Begins | 15 | Exit the tunnel |
| First Steps | 15 | Craft your first tool (wrench) |
| Home Sweet Home | 15 | Place first furniture in your van |
| Past of Two Forks | 15 | Reach the top of the Fire Lookout |
| Cross the Chasm | 30 | Repair the broken bridge |
| Ultimate Upgrade | 30 | Install the Solar Roof blueprint |

---

## 🧭 Collectibles (8 Achievements – hardest)

- **Gnome Sweet Gnome (80)**: Collect all 5 gnomes (see coordinates in our Easter Egg guide).
- **Gacha Completion (80)**: Get all 3 bobbleheads from the vending machine.
- **Cairnivore (80)**: Stack all 8 stone piles across the map.
- **Camper Van Gogh (80)**: Collect all 4 hidden paintings.
- **Scrap Terminator (30)**: Recycle 100 junk items.
- **Cap Collector (30)**: Gather 20 bottle caps.
- **Blueprint Master (30)**: Download every signal tower blueprint.
- **Note Scholar (30)**: Read all 4 hidden notes.

---

## 🔨 Building & Customization (6 Achievements)

- **Decked Out (30)**: Craft all vehicle upgrade parts.
- **Don’t Break the Camel’s Back (30)**: Reach maximum roof load.
- **Space Magician (30)**: Build 3 or more storeys on your van.
- **Energy Freedom (30)**: Install solar, wind, and hydro simultaneously.
- **Fashionista (15)**: Change clothes in all 6 colours.
- **Chromatic Confusion (15)**: Paint your van 10 times.

## 🧑‍🌾 Survival & Life (6 Achievements)

- **Master Chef (30)**: Cook all 6 recipes.
- **Green Thumb (30)**: Harvest 30 crops.
- **Fur‑Ever Friend (15)**: Adopt a pet.
- **Campfire Guardian (30)**: Light all 8 campfires.
- **Angler (30)**: Catch 10 fish.
- **Night Owl (15)**: Spend 10 nights outdoors.

---

## 🥷 Hidden Achievements (6)

1. **Skyward (30)**: Ride the hot air balloon at the Lighthouse.  
2. **Hidden Chest (30)**: Chop roots at (1200, 800).  
3. **Vegetarian (15)**: Never eat meat (automatic).  
4. **Rabbit Friend (15)**: Feed & pet 10 rabbits.  
5. **Rainy Camp (15)**: Sit by a campfire in the rain for 5 min.  
6. **Ultimate Explorer (80)**: Uncover the entire map, light all campfires, unlock all towers.

---

## 🚀 15‑Hour Speed Route

- **Hours 1‑3**: Starting zone – wrench, bridge, first gnome.  
- **Hours 4‑6**: Right of bridge – vending machine, sawmill, rooftop platform.  
- **Hours 7‑10**: Northern forest – wood paradise, solar panels, cairns.  
- **Hours 11‑15**: Southern coast – pet, fishing, lighthouse, balloon.
