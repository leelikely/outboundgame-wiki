⏱️ **One of the most common questions** from new players is: “How long does it take to finish Outbound?” The answer depends entirely on your playstyle.

Here’s what we’ve gathered from our own playthroughs and community feedback:

## 🏃 Main Story Speedrun – 15–20 Hours
If you focus strictly on the critical path — repairing bridges, unlocking signal towers, and confronting the final challenge — you can finish the campaign in **15–20 hours**. This approach requires ignoring side quests, skipping deep exploration, and minimising resource hoarding.

## 🌿 Relaxed Exploration – 30–40 Hours
This is the most common playstyle. While pushing the main story, you’ll also wander off to collect materials, build a cosy van, and complete optional tasks. The slower pace lets you soak in the atmosphere, and most players land in the **30–40 hour** range.

## 🧭 Completionist (100%) – 50–60 Hours
Unlocking every blueprint, discovering every hidden location, and collecting all items can push your playtime to **50–60 hours**. Patience is key, but seeing that 100% progress feels incredibly rewarding.

## 📌 Extra Factors
- **Skill level**: Newcomers may need more time, veterans less.
- **Co‑op**: Playing with friends can speed up resource gathering but might also add distraction.
- **Game version**: Future updates may alter mission lengths.

> 💡 **Keep in mind**: Outbound is an open‑world sandbox. Even after the credits roll, you can continue building, exploring, and living your best van life. Don’t stress about the clock — the journey matters most.
