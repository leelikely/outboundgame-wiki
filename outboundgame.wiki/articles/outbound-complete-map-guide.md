# Outbound Complete Map Guide

Navigate the world like a pro. This guide covers all four major regions, with coordinates, resources, and secrets.

---

## 🗺️ Using the In‑Game Map

- Press **M** to open the map.
- 🟡 Yellow flashing dots = unvisited signal towers.
- ⚪ White dots = activated signal towers.
- 🔥 Campfire icons = fast‑travel points (must be lit first).
- Click anywhere to set a navigation marker.

---

## 📍 Region 1: Starting Area (Tunnel Exit → Broken Bridge)

**Signal Towers**: 1  
- Coordinates: (500, 300)  
- Blueprints: Wrench, Construction Hammer, Recycler

**Campfires** (2):  
- Gravel Camp (200, -100)  
- Lakeside Camp (800, -200)

**Resources**:  
- Scrap metal: under street lights along the main road.  
- Wood: scattered near the lake.  
- Junk: 3 fixed spawns (one per camp).

**Secrets**:  
- A fishing gnome sits on a chair at the Lakeside Camp.  
- Trash bins can drop bottle caps (up to 3).

---

## 🌉 Region 2: Right of the Broken Bridge

**Signal Towers**: 2  
1. (1800, 400) → Sawmill, Gardening, Food Processor  
2. Forest edge → Rooftop Platform, Furniture Set

**Campfires** (2):  
- Forest Entrance Camp (1500, 400)  
- Treehouse Camp (1700, 600)

**Resources**:  
- Wood: small trees at forest edge (2‑3 per tree).  
- Fiber: abundant in reed clumps near the lake.  
- Blueberries: bushes in the meadow.

**Secrets**:  
- Vending Machine at (1800, 200): exchange bottle caps for dashboard toys.  
- Treehouse contains 3 hidden notes.

---

## 🌲 Region 3: Northern Forest

**Signal Towers**: 2  
1. Fire Lookout Tower (1500, 1200) → Solar Panel, Wind Turbine, Watering Can  
2. Logging Camp (1800, 1500) → Sofa, Storage Lockers, Advanced Workbench

**Campfires** (2):  
- Lookout Camp (1400, 1100)  
- Logging Camp (1800, 1500)

**Resources**:  
- Wood: dense forest (5 per large tree).  
- Ginger: red flowers in deep woods.  
- Mushrooms: under tree shade.

**Secrets**:  
- Pet Training Manual on the top floor of the Fire Lookout.  
- Rabbit herds: feed them berries to make them follow you.  
- "Wood Paradise" at (1245, 789): massive daily tree spawns.

---

## 🏖️ Region 4: Southern Coast (Full Release)

**Signal Towers**: 2  
1. Beach Camp (2000, -800) → Hydro Generator, Greenhouse, Fishing Rod  
2. Lighthouse (2500, 1800) → Pet House, Sewing Machine, Ultimate Van Blueprint

**Campfires** (2):  
- Beach Camp  
- Lighthouse Camp

**Resources**:  
- Shells on the beach.  
- Seawater (requires purifier).  
- Rare plants near the reef.

**Secrets**:  
- Solar Roof blueprint at the top of the Lighthouse.  
- Cat & Dog Cabin (2200, 500): adopt a pet.  
- Shipwreck (2300, -1200): tons of junk and bottle caps.
