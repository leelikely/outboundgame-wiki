🧱 Everwood Planks are a late‑game bottleneck. Here’s every reliable way to stockpile them.

## 🛠️ Method 1: Crafting (Best Overall)
- Chop **dead trees** for Everwood Logs.
- Process **2 logs → 1 Everwood Plank** at the Sawmill.
- This is the most consistent and scalable method.

## 🏚️ Method 2: Scavenging (Low Yield)
Explore construction sites, warehouses, and large ruins. There’s a small chance to find ready‑made Everwood Planks on the ground.

## 🪓 Method 3: Dismantle High‑End Furniture
Some abandoned buildings contain furniture made of Everwood: large tables, wardrobes, beds. Use your axe to break them — each gives **2–4 Everwood Planks**. This is far more efficient than scavenging.

## 💱 Method 4: Trading (Late Game)
A hidden NPC in the forest will trade **10 Regular Planks** for **1 Everwood Plank**. Use this only if you have an overflow of normal planks.

## 📋 Recommended Strategy
- During normal wood runs, always chop dead trees to build a backlog of Everwood Logs.
- When you need a big batch, go furniture‑hunting in high‑end ruins.
- Combine crafting and dismantling for a steady, reliable supply.

> 🏆 With these methods, the days of being stuck at a bridge are over.
