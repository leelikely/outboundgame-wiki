# Outbound Frequently Asked Questions

Got a problem? Check these answers first.

---

## 🔋 My van ran out of power. What now?
Open the hood, place wood or fiber inside. 12 wood will fully recharge an empty battery.

## 📦 My backpack is too heavy. Help!
Move materials into the van’s storage lockers. Only carry what you need for the current task.

## 🗺️ I can’t find a signal tower.
Press M to open the map. Yellow markers show undiscovered towers.

## 🏃 How do I travel faster?
Hold Shift to sprint on foot, or unlock engine upgrades for your van later.

## 📁 Will my demo progress carry over to the full game?
No, the full release requires a fresh start.
