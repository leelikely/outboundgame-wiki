🌉 Broken bridges are the main progression gates in Outbound. They block access to new regions, and every beginner eventually gets stuck wondering what materials they need. Here's your complete reference.

---

## 🔧 How Bridge Repair Works

All broken bridges follow the same rules:

1. 🧱 Collect the required **Everwood Planks**.
2. 🚶 Walk up to the bridge and press `E`.
3. ⚙️ Repair is automatic — no tools needed.
4. ✅ The bridge becomes a permanent road.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Everwood Planks must be **crafted** at the Sawmill using Everwood Logs (2 logs → 1 plank). You cannot pick them up directly.

---

## 🗺️ All Broken Bridges (v0.8.3)

### 🌉 1. Northern Bridge (First Priority)

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| 📍 **Location** | North of your starting area |
| 🧱 **Materials** | **10 Everwood Planks** (20 Everwood Logs) |
| 🔓 **Unlocks** | 🏖️ **The Coast** region |
| ⭐ **Importance** | Must‑fix — gates the entire second region |

### 🌉 2. Eastern Bridge

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| 📍 **Location** | East of your starting area |
| 🧱 **Materials** | **8 Everwood Planks** (16 Everwood Logs) |
| 🔓 **Unlocks** | 🌻 **Sunbeam Acres Farm** |
| ⭐ **Importance** | Key food source — wheat fields and carrots |

### 🌉 3. Southern Bridge

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| 📍 **Location** | South of your starting area |
| 🧱 **Materials** | **15 Everwood Planks** (30 Everwood Logs) |
| 🔓 **Unlocks** | 🏜️ **The Canyons** region |
| ⭐ **Importance** | Newest region — fresh resources and blueprints |

### 🌁 4. Central Drawbridge

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| 📍 **Location** | Middle of the river — connects to a small island |
| 🧱 **Materials** | **None** — lower it from the far side after repairing the Northern or Southern bridge |
| 🔓 **Unlocks** | 🏝️ **Central Island** — hidden Signal Tower and rare chests |
| ⭐ **Importance** | Optional but rewarding |

## 🪓 How to Farm Everwood Planks Efficiently

| Method | Yield | Reliability |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| 🪓 Chop dead trees | 3–5 Everwood Logs per tree | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 🏚️ Scavenge ruins | Small chance of ready‑made planks | ⭐⭐ |
| 🪑 Dismantle high‑end furniture | 2–4 planks per item | ⭐⭐⭐ |

---

## 🎯 Recommended Repair Order

1. 🥇 **Eastern Bridge** → Secure a reliable food supply at Sunbeam Acres
2. 🥈 **Northern Bridge** → Access the Coast for advanced blueprints
3. 🥉 **Southern Bridge** → Explore the latest Canyons content
4. 🏅 **Central Drawbridge** → Bonus loot and collectibles

> 🌉 **Pro tip:** Gather all the Everwood Logs you need **before** walking to the bridge. Nothing's more frustrating than arriving short by one plank and having to trek back.
