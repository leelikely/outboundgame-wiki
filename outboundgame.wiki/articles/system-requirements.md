# Outbound System Requirements

Wondering if your setup can handle the open road? Below you'll find the official **minimum** and **recommended** specifications for Outbound on PC, straight from Square Glade Games.

---

## 💻 PC System Requirements

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| 🖥️ **OS** | Windows 10 (64‑bit) | Windows 11 (64‑bit) |
| ⚙️ **Processor** | Intel Core i3‑10100F **or** AMD Ryzen 3 3100 | Intel Core i3‑12100F **or** AMD Ryzen 7 1700 (or better) |
| 🧠 **Memory** | 4 GB RAM | 8 GB RAM |
| 🎮 **Graphics** | NVIDIA GTX 1050 **or** AMD RX 570 | NVIDIA RTX 3060 **or** AMD RX 7600 XT (or better) |
| 📐 **DirectX** | Version 11 | Version 12 |
| 💾 **Storage** | 16 GB available space | 16 GB available space |
| 🔊 **Sound Card** | DirectX compatible | DirectX compatible |

---

## 🎮 Controller Support

Prefer a gamepad for your road trip? Outbound fully supports:

- 🎮 **Xbox Controllers** (wired & wireless)
- 🎮 **PlayStation Controllers** (DualShock 4 & DualSense)

Just plug in and play — no additional setup required.

---

## 🧭 What Do These Specs Mean for You?

**Minimum specs** will get you on the road at lower settings, perfect for older gaming laptops or budget desktops. Expect smooth 30+ FPS at 1080p with settings on Low to Medium.

**Recommended specs** unlock the full visual experience: lush forests, dynamic weather, and detailed van interiors at a steady 60 FPS on High settings.

The 16 GB storage footprint is refreshingly modest by modern standards — no need to clear out your entire library for one game.

---

## ✅ Quick Compatibility Checklist

- [ ] 64‑bit Windows 10 or 11
- [ ] At least 4 GB RAM (8 GB ideal)
- [ ] Dedicated GPU with 2 GB+ VRAM
- [ ] 16 GB free disk space
- [ ] Xbox or PlayStation controller (optional but cozy)
