🏚️ Abandoned structures in Outbound aren't just decoration — they're packed with supplies. Many beginners breeze past, opening a single chest and leaving, missing blueprints, rare materials, and entire hidden rooms. Looting houses is the single fastest way to jump‑start your resources, and it's far more efficient than grinding trees. Here's my complete sweep guide.

## 🎒 Before You Head Out

| Essential Item | Why |
|----------------|-----|
| 🪓 **Axe** | Break furniture, doors, crates |
| 🎒 **2+ free backpack slots** | You'll fill up fast |
| 🔥 **One torch** | Cellars and attics are dark and may spawn creatures |

No need for heavy weapons — most houses are empty or have only a few weak enemies.

## 🌲 The Outdoors Region: Must‑Loot Buildings

### 1. Woods Cabin

| Location | Key Finds |
|----------|-----------|
| 🏠 **Kitchen cupboards** | Canned food, clean water |
| 🔧 **Beside the workbench** | 2–3 Rope, a handful of Nails (fixed spawn) |
| 🛏️ **Bedroom nightstand** | Small chance of Download Vouchers |
| 📦 **Corner crates** | Basic mats: Stone, Planks |

🔍 **Easily missed:** A storage shed sits behind the cabin. Most players never check it — it frequently holds Leather and Cloth.

### 2. Mountain Outpost

| Location | Key Finds |
|----------|-----------|
| 📦 **Three large storage crates** | Loads of Stone and Iron Ore — the easiest early Iron source |
| 🗄️ **Wall shelves** | A spare Pickaxe with low durability |
| 🖥️ **Upstairs desk** | Rare blueprint fragments (random) |
| 🛢️ **Outside, near the oil drums** | Occasional Gasoline spawn |

⚠️ This building alone can save you 15 minutes of mining. Prioritise it.

### 3. Sunbeam Acres Farm (Requires Eastern Bridge)

| Location | Key Finds |
|----------|-----------|
| 🌾 **Barn** | Planks, Nails, tool parts |
| 🍳 **Kitchen** | Food, seeds |
| 🔒 **Basement locked chest** | Rare materials and Download Vouchers |
| 🥕 **Farming plots** | Harvestable carrots and wheat |

🐗 **Caution:** Wild boars patrol the area. Clear them before looting — losing a full backpack to a charge is heartbreaking.

## 🏖️ The Coast Region: Must‑Loot Buildings

### 1. Paws & Whiskers Lodge

| Location | Key Finds |
|----------|-----------|
| 🏨 **Front desk** | Food, drinks |
| 🛏️ **Guest rooms** | Cloth, clothing, occasional Leather |
| 🐕 **Backyard kennel** | A hidden chest! |
| 🍳 **Kitchen** | Abundant cooking ingredients |

Of course, the real prize here is adopting a pet dog — but grab the loot first.

### 2. Coffee Farm Manager's Hut

| Location | Key Finds |
|----------|-----------|
| ☕ **Coffee beans** | Bulk supply — used in high‑end cooking |
| 📦 **Storage shelves** | Glass, bottles, Rope |
| 🏠 **Attic chest** | Rare decorative blueprints |
| 🔧 **Tool shed (behind house)** | Advanced tool components |

## 🧠 Universal Looting Rules

No matter which building you're raiding, these habits will ensure you never miss loot:

- 🔄 **Circle the building** — back doors, side sheds, and junk piles often hide the best stuff.
- 🪓 **Destroy all furniture** — tables, chairs, and cupboards drop materials when broken. It adds up.
- 🕳️ **Check basements and attics** — that's where chests, blueprints, and rare items spawn.
- 🗄️ **Open every drawer** — nightstands and desks frequently contain Download Vouchers and small parts.
- 🖼️ **Look behind wall paintings** — some conceal a safe with rare loot.

## 🗺️ Looting Priority (Highest ROI First)

| Priority | Building | Why |
|----------|----------|-----|
| 🥇 **1** | Mountain Outpost | Scores you Iron Ore for the Furnace |
| 🥈 **2** | Woods Cabin | Rope, food, and basic supplies |
| 🥉 **3** | Sunbeam Acres Farm | Food security and seeds |
| 🏅 **4** | Coast Region buildings | Advanced materials for late‑game gear |

## ⏰ Final Warning

Always watch the clock. Getting caught in the wilderness at night with a backpack full of rare loot is a disaster waiting to happen. Set out at dawn, finish your raid by midday, and return safely to your van. A successful scavenger trip is one where you live to store the goods.
