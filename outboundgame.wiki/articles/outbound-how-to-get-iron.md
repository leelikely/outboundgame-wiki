⚙️ Iron marks the transition from surviving to thriving. Once you can produce Iron Ingots, you'll unlock advanced tools, reinforced van parts, and fortified buildings. Here's the complete roadmap.

---

## 🔧 Prerequisites (Must Complete First)

| Requirement | Where to Get It |
|-------------|-----------------|
| ⛏️ **Pickaxe II blueprint** | Fire Lookout Signal Tower (2 Download Vouchers) |
| 🔥 **Metal Smelter blueprint** | The Large Treehouse Signal Tower |
| 🪵 **Fuel** | Wood, fiber, or coal — I recommend wood for longest burn time |

> ⚠️ Pickaxe I cannot mine iron. You **must** upgrade to Pickaxe II.

---

## 🗺️ Where to Find Iron Ore

### 🥇 Primary Location: Open‑Pit Quarry (near the vehicle lift)
The single best iron source in the game. All ore is **exposed on the surface** — no caves, no darkness. Just northeast of the vehicle lift, follow the dirt path uphill and you'll see the rust‑coloured rocks.

### 🥈 Secondary: Coast Cliff Faces
Scattered along the northern cliffs of the Coast region. Convenient to grab while exploring.

### 🥉 Tertiary: Mountain Caves
Small caves in the northern mountains hold rich iron deposits. Bring torches — visibility is terrible underground.

### 🔍 How to Identify Iron Ore
Iron ore has **distinct dark red rust patches** on its surface. If it looks grey, it's regular stone — move on.

---

## 🔥 Smelting Iron Ingots

1. 🏠 Return to your van with raw iron ore.
2. 🔥 Approach the Metal Smelter and press `E`.
3. ⬆️ Place **Iron Ore** in the top input slot.
4. ⬇️ Add **fuel** (wood recommended) to the bottom slot.
5. ▶️ Click **"Start Smelting"** — wait ~5 seconds.
6. 📤 **2 Iron Ore = 1 Iron Ingot**. Collect from the output slot.

## 🛠️ What Iron Ingots Are Used For

| Category | Items Unlocked |
|----------|----------------|
| 🔧 **Advanced Tools** | Iron Axe, Iron Pickaxe, Iron Sickle |
| ⚔️ **Weapons** | Iron Sword, Iron Spear |
| 🚐 **Van Upgrades** | Reinforced shell, expanded storage |
| 🏗️ **Construction** | Metal walls, metal doors — much safer base |

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- 🧺 Bring enough ore for **10+ ingots per trip** — smelt in bulk while you do other tasks.
- 🏭 The Smelter can process **20 ore at once** — load it up and walk away.
- 🔄 Iron ore **respawns** over time — it's a renewable resource, so mine freely.

> 🏆 Once you've established a steady iron supply, the entire mid‑game opens up. Prioritise the quarry, upgrade your pickaxe, and smelt in bulk.
