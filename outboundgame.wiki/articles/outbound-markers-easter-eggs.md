## ⚡ Fast‑Travel Route (3 hours to clear the map)

### Phase 1 – Starting Zone (0‑30 min)
Spawn → collect scrap & wood → 1st Signal Tower (wrench etc.) → light Gravel & Lakeside campfires → repair bridge.

### Phase 2 – Right of Bridge (30‑60 min)
Bridge → Vending Machine → light Forest Entrance & Treehouse camps → unlock Sawmill & Roof Platform.

### Phase 3 – Northern Forest (60‑120 min)
Treehouse camp → Wood Paradise (50+ wood) → light Lookout & Logging camps → Fire Lookout blueprints.

### Phase 4 – Southern Coast (120‑180 min)
Bridge → Beach camp → Shipwreck → Lighthouse → Cat & Dog Cabin → finish all achievements.
