# Outbound Game Overview

**Developer:** Square Glade Games  
**Genre:** Open‑world survival, base‑building, sandbox  
**Launch Date:** May 11, 2026 (Steam, Epic Games Store, Xbox)  
**Other Platforms:** Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5 (May 14)  

---

## A Hopeful Road Trip into the Future

Welcome to *Outbound*, a tranquil survival‑building experience that trades tension for tea‑time. Created by Square Glade Games, the title drops you into a utopian near‑future where lush forests, sun‑drenched coasts, and rolling plains set the stage for your greatest adventure: life on the road.

Your camper van isn’t just transport — it’s a canvas. Build upward and outward, turning a modest vehicle into a rolling home complete with crafting stations, rooftop gardens, and cozy nooks. Power everything with clean energy (solar, wind, water) and grow your own food. No hunger bars, no mandatory quests, no enemies at the gate. Just **you, your van, and the open world.**

---

## The “Dodge the Leviathan” Launch Story

Originally aiming for April 23, 2026, the team postponed to May 14 for extra polish. Then came unexpected news: *Subnautica 2* had claimed the exact same day. Rather than compete with a leviathan, Square Glade Games made the brilliant decision to **release three days early — May 11, 2026.**

If you’re on **Steam, Epic Games Store, or Xbox**, your road trip begins immediately. PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Switch 2 drivers hop in on May 14.

---

## Why Outbound Stands Out

- 🚐 **Transformable Camper Van** – Expand your van with floors, walls, and roofs. The modular building system lets you shape your home on wheels.
- 🌱 **Self‑Sufficient Living** – Farms, cooking stations, and material processing let you live off the land. Every resource has a purpose.
- ☀️ **Clean Energy** – Solar panels, wind turbines, and water generators keep your lights on without a drop of fuel.
- 🐕 **Adoptable Pets** – Because every journey is better with a furry friend.
- 🧘 **No Pressure** – No survival meters, no ticking clock. The world waits for you.

---

## Language Support & Accessibility

The game supports **English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese**, and more. Clear tutorials and a friendly interface make it a great entry point for newcomers to the genre.
