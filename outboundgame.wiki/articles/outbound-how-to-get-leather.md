🪶 Leather is a mid‑game bottleneck. You'll need it for hiking boots, upgraded backpacks, belts, and several important crafting recipes. Here's how to stockpile it efficiently.

---

## 🏹 Method 1: Hunting &amp; Tanning (Best Overall)

This is the most reliable, scalable method.

1. **Find animals**: Deer, rabbits, and foxes spawn in forested areas — especially around **Fire Lookout** and **Sunbeam Acres Farm**.
2. **Hunt them**: Use a bow for ranged attacks; it's safer and preserves your health. An axe works if you're desperate.
3. **Collect Hide**: Each animal drops **1–2 Hide**.
4. **Build a Tanning Rack**: Download the blueprint from a Signal Tower, then craft it inside your van.
5. **Tan the Hide**: Place Hide on the Tanning Rack and wait a few seconds. **1 Hide = 1 Leather**.

> 🎯 **Pro tip**: Approach animals from behind, crouch to reduce noise, and aim for the head with a bow. One clean shot will bring them down.

---

## 🏚️ Method 2: Scavenge Abandoned Buildings (Emergency)

While exploring ruins, cabins, and warehouses, check containers and shelves. Ready‑made leather sometimes spawns in:
- **Woods Cabin** (deep forest)
- **Mountain Outpost** (northern hills)

This method is inconsistent but useful in the early game before you have a Tanning Rack.

---

## ♻️ Method 3: Recycle Old Clothing (Supplemental)

Torn jackets, trousers, and boots occasionally contain leather. Drop them into your **Recycler** — each item has a small chance of yielding **1 Leather**. It's not efficient, but it's better than discarding them.

---

## 📍 Best Hunting Spots &amp; Times

| Factor | Best Choice |
|--------|-------------|
| 🌍 **Location** | Forest south of Fire Lookout — highest deer density |
| 🕐 **Time** | Dawn and dusk — animals are most active |
| 🌧️ **Weather** | Clear days — animals hide during storms |

> 🧵 With a steady supply of leather, you'll breeze through mid‑game crafting. Focus on hunting during dawn hours and tan your hides in batches while you do other tasks.
