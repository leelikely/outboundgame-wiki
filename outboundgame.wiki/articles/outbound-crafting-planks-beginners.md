🪚 Planks are a fundamental building block, yet many new players get stuck before repairing the bridge. Here’s exactly what to do.

## 🔓 Unlocking the Sawmill
1. Gather **Litter** (rubbish) scattered around your van.
2. Build a **Recycler** at the back of your van (free).
3. Put litter into the Recycler to get **Download Vouchers**.
4. Drive to the **first Signal Tower** and download the **Sawmill** blueprint (costs 1 voucher).

## 🏗️ Building the Sawmill
Enter build mode inside your van, find the Sawmill under “Processing”, and place it on a free spot.

## 🔨 Crafting Planks
1. Collect **2 Regular Wood Logs**.
2. Stand next to the Sawmill and press `E`.
3. Drag logs into the left input slot.
4. Select **“Plank”** on the right, then click **Craft**.
5. Wait a few seconds — **2 logs = 1 plank**.

## ⚡ Efficiency Trick
You can load many logs at once. Drop **10 logs** into the input, queue 5 planks, and let the machine work while you do other tasks. The Sawmill doesn’t require power!
