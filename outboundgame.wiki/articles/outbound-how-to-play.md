Follow these steps to get started in Outbound.

1. **After spawning**, pick up litter around your camper van.
2. **Build a Recycler**: open the van’s back door, enter build mode, and craft it (free).
3. **Earn Download Vouchers**: place the litter into the Recycler to get vouchers.
4. **Unlock the Axe**: drive to the nearest blue Signal Tower, interact with the computer, and download the **Axe I** blueprint (1 voucher).
5. **Craft the Axe** back at your van in build mode.
6. **Gather wood**: use the axe to chop trees and collect logs.
7. **Unlock the Sawmill**: gather more litter, exchange for vouchers, and download the Sawmill blueprint from the Signal Tower.
8. **Craft Planks**: build the Sawmill and convert logs into planks.
9. **Repair the bridge**: collect 10 Everwood Planks and fix the broken bridge to the north, opening a new area.
10. **Keep exploring** – unlock more Signal Towers, get new blueprints, and upgrade your tools and camper.
