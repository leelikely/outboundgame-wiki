👥 **Yes, you can road‑trip with friends!** Outbound currently allows up to **4 players** in the same world.

## 🔧 How to Start a Multiplayer Session
- Invite Steam friends directly, or
- Join a public room and meet new travelers.

**Important:** There are no dedicated servers yet. The host creates a local lobby, and if the host disconnects, everyone is kicked.

## 🚐 Sharing a World (and a Van)
All players inhabit the same map and **share the same camper van**. This makes cooperation essential:
- One person drives while another cooks.
- Someone gathers wood, another crafts tools.
- Items can be traded or dropped for teammates.

⚠️ **Building permissions are open to everyone.** Any player can modify or dismantle parts of the van, so it’s best to play only with people you trust.

## ⚔️ Difficulty Scaling
When more players join, enemies gain extra health and appear in greater numbers. Don’t assume strength in numbers comes without challenge!

## 🎒 Personal Inventory
Each player’s backpack is **separate** — nobody can steal what you’re carrying. Only items placed in the shared van storage become communal.

> ✅ **Pro tip**: Assign clear roles (driver, builder, gatherer) to avoid chaos and build your dream mobile base faster.
