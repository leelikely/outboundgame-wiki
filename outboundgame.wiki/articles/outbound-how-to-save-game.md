💾 One of the most frequent questions from new players is: *"How do I save my progress?"* Outbound doesn't have a big "Save" button, but its saving system is actually very reliable — once you understand how it works.

## 🔄 Auto‑Save System (Primary Method)

Outbound relies on **automatic saving**. The game silently saves your progress at these key moments:

- ✅ When you **enter or exit your camper van**
- ✅ When you **download a new blueprint**
- ✅ When you **repair a bridge** or complete a major objective
- ✅ When you **collect an important item**
- ✅ When you **quit to the main menu**

I've tested this extensively — auto‑save fires very frequently. In normal gameplay, you'll rarely lose more than a minute or two of progress.

## ✋ How to Force a Manual Save

There's no dedicated "Save Game" button, but these two tricks will force an immediate save:

### 🚪 Method 1: Enter & Exit Your Van

Walk inside your camper van, then immediately walk back out. The game auto‑saves every time you enter the van. This is my go‑to method during long exploration runs.

### 🖥️ Method 2: Quit to Main Menu

Press `Esc`, select **"Quit to Main Menu"**, and the game will save before closing. This is the most reliable method — I use it before every major crafting session or dangerous expedition.

## ⚠️ Important Tips & Common Pitfalls

| ❌ Avoid This | ✅ Do This Instead |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Closing the game via Task Manager or Alt+F4 | Always quit through the in‑game menu |
| Assuming a friend's world saves your progress | Only the **host's** world progress is saved |
| Forgetting to back up your saves | Periodically copy your save folder as a safety net |

## 📁 Save File Location (Windows)

Your save files are stored locally on your PC. To find them:

`C:\Users\YourUsername\AppData\LocalLow\Square Glade Games\Outbound`

💡 **Backup tip:** Copy this entire folder to a safe location every few play sessions. If your save ever becomes corrupted (which can happen in early‑access games), you'll be glad you did.

## 👥 Multiplayer Note

In co‑op, **only the host's world progress is saved**. Items in your personal backpack are saved regardless, but if you're a guest, don't count on the world state persisting after you leave.

> 🧠 **My rule of thumb**: Before crafting expensive items, entering a dangerous cave, or logging off — walk into your van first. Two seconds of precaution saves hours of heartache.
