🌲 Everwood Planks are the upgraded version of regular planks. You’ll need them for the northern bridge and advanced workstations.

## 🪓 Step 1: Collect Everwood Logs
Chop **dead trees** (grey trunks). Each felled tree drops **3–5 Everwood Logs**. Do not bother with the large green living evergreens — they are indestructible.

## 🏭 Step 2: Use the Sawmill
1. Return to your van and stand beside the Sawmill.
2. Press `E` to open the interface.
3. Drag Everwood Logs into the left input slot.
4. Select **“Everwood Plank”** from the recipe list.
5. Click **Craft** — **2 Everwood Logs = 1 Everwood Plank**.

## 🧮 Quantity Needed
To repair the northern bridge, you’ll need **10 Everwood Planks**, which equals **20 Everwood Logs**. Stockpile them beforehand to avoid backtracking.

## 💡 Smarter Crafting
Load the Sawmill with a batch of logs, queue up all the planks, and go do another task while they process. The Sawmill works continuously without fuel!
