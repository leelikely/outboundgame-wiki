## 🛠️ Method 1: Crafting (Recommended)

1. Follow the steps to unlock and build the Sawmill.
2. Place regular wood logs into the Sawmill to produce planks (2 logs → 1 plank).
3. This is the most consistent and bulk-friendly method.

## 🏚️ Method 2: Scavenging

- Explore abandoned houses, warehouses, and roadside ruins.
- Look for plank piles or wooden crates lying on the ground.
- Breaking wooden crates has a high chance of dropping planks.
- Best used early‑game when you don’t have a Sawmill yet.
