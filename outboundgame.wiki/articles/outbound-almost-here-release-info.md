# Outbound is almost here! Here is some important release info

---

## 📅 Release Dates

| Platform | Date |
|----------|------|
| Steam, Epic Games Store, Xbox Series X|S | **Monday, May 11** |
| PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 1 & 2 | **Thursday, May 14** |

---

## 🕑 Global Release Time

Outbound will unlock on both dates at **2:00 PM UTC**. Consult the time zone map above to find your local launch hour.

---

## 💵 Pricing

The standard digital price is **$24.99 / €24.99 / £19.99**. Regional equivalents are available on each storefront.

![Regional pricing chart](/images/price-chart.jpg)

---

## 🚌 School Bus Adventures DLC

Alongside the base game, the **School Bus Adventures DLC** will launch, adding a fully playable school bus vehicle.  
Price: **$2.99 / €2.99 / £2.49**.

---

## 🏷️ Launch Discounts

- **10% launch discount** on all platforms.
- **Extra 5% bundle discount** on Steam when you combine the base game, DLC, and original soundtrack (Digital Deluxe Edition).

---

## 🌟 What You Can Expect from Outbound

- A super‑relaxing cozy exploration game  
- Solo or up to 4‑player co‑op  
- A cute dog companion 🐾  
- Build your dream mobile home  
- 4 maps with diverse biomes  
- 3 vehicles + 1 DLC vehicle  
- 175+ hand‑placed collectibles  
- 250+ furniture and decoration pieces  
- 50+ base building pieces  
- 100+ unique resources and materials  
- 32 achievements (including platinum on PS5)  
- Full controller support and key remapping  
- Various accessibility features

## 🎮 Xbox Game Pass

We're thrilled to announce that **Outbound is coming to Xbox Game Pass on May 11!**  
The School Bus Adventures DLC is not included in the subscription but can be purchased separately.
