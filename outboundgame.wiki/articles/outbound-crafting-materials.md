# Outbound Crafting Materials – Complete List & Where to Find Them

Here’s every crafting material in the game, where to get it, and what you’ll need it for.

| Material | Source | Primary Use |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Wood | Chop trees, pick up from ground | Fuel, building, crafting planks |
| Scrap Metal | Under street lights, trash bins, campsites | Tools, metal parts |
| Junk / Trash | Campsites, roadsides, bins | Recycler → Download Tickets |
| Fiber | Reeds, plants | Fillings, sofas, ropes |
| Berries / Blueberries | Forage, farming | Eating, jam, seeds |
| Planks | Sawmill (2 logs = 1 plank) | Building houses, furniture |
| Stone | Forage | Decoration (no practical use in demo) |
| Ginger | Red flowers in forests | Healing (medical pack) |
| Bottle Caps | Trash bins, campsites, quests | Vending machine decorations |
| Download Tickets | Recycler (2 junk = 1 ticket) | Unlock blueprints at signal towers |
