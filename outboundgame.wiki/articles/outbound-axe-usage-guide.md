---
title: Axe Usage Guide – Chopping Trees in Outbound
date: 2026-05-12
category: Guides
excerpt: Master the axe mechanics: from equipping it to efficiently chopping different tree types and collecting logs.
---

**Prerequisite:** The Axe is crafted and placed on your hotbar.

1. Press the number key (1‑9) assigned to the Axe to equip it.
2. Find a **dead tree** (Everwood) or a regular tree.
3. Walk up to the trunk until the **“Chop”** prompt appears in the center of the screen.
4. Hold down the left mouse button to chop continuously.
5. Once the tree falls, it will drop 3‑5 logs of the corresponding type.
6. Walk over the dropped logs and press the left mouse button to pick them up.
7. ⚠️ The axe can only fell dead trees and regular trees – it cannot cut living Everwood trees (the large green ones).
