🗼 The Signal Tower is your primary source of new blueprints — and the first one is critical. Yet many beginners wander aimlessly, unsure where to go. Follow this guide and you'll be downloading your first tools in minutes.

---

## 📍 Exact Location

The first Signal Tower sits at the base of **Fire Lookout** hill, just southeast of your spawn point.

### 🧭 Route from Your Van:
1. 🚐 Face **southeast** from your orange camper van.
2. 🛤️ Follow the dirt road — you'll see a tall hill ahead.
3. ⛰️ The Fire Lookout tower sits at the very top.
4. 🗼 The **Signal Tower is halfway up the hill**, beside the road — **not at the summit**.

It's a tall metal structure with a small white building and a computer terminal at its base. You can't miss it.

---

## 💳 Before You Go: Prepare Download Vouchers

Download Vouchers are the currency for unlocking blueprints. Without them, the Signal Tower is useless.

### How to Get Vouchers:
1. 🔍 Collect **Litter** (white bags) scattered around your van.
2. ♻️ Feed them into the **Recycler** at the back of your van.
3. 🔄 **2 Litter = 1 Download Voucher**.

> 🎯 **Bring at least 3 vouchers** on your first visit — enough for all the essential tool blueprints.

---

## 🖥️ How to Use the Signal Tower

1. Walk up to the computer terminal beneath the tower.
2. ⌨️ Press `E` to open the interface.
3. 📋 Browse the available blueprints.
4. 🖱️ Select one and click **"Download"** — costs 1 voucher.
5. ✅ The blueprint is permanently added to your Workbench.

---

## 🎯 Priority Download Order

Don't waste vouchers on decorations early on. Here's what to unlock first:

| Priority | Blueprint | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| 🥇 | 🪓 **Axe** | Can't chop trees without it |
| 🥈 | ⛏️ **Pickaxe** | Unlocks stone and iron mining |
| 🥉 | 🌾 **Sickle** | Triples fiber yield |

> 🪑 Save furniture and cosmetic blueprints for later — tools come first.

| Question | Answer |
