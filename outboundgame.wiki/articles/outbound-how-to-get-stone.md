🪨 Stone is the foundation of your early game. From your first axe and campfire to late‑game van expansions, you'll burn through more stone than you expect. Here's how to gather it efficiently.

---

## 🤲 Method 1: Hand‑Picking Small Rocks (0–15 min)

**Best for:** Your first axe and campfire.

- 🔍 Look for small white pebbles near the van, riverbanks, and forest clearings.
- 🖱️ Walk up and click — each yields **1 Stone**.

| ✅ Pros | ❌ Cons |
|---------|---------|
| Zero tools required | Very low yield |
| Available immediately | Scattered and time‑consuming |

---

## ⛏️ Method 2: Pickaxe Mining (Mid‑Game, Best ROI)

Once you've unlocked the **Pickaxe I** blueprint at the Fire Lookout (1 Download Voucher), your stone income skyrockets.

1. 🔨 Craft the Pickaxe at your Workbench (2 Wood + 1 Stone).
2. 🪨 Locate **large grey rock formations** — they're unmistakable.
3. 🖱️ Hold left mouse to mine — each large rock yields **3 Stone**.
4. 🔄 Rocks respawn roughly 10 minutes after you leave the area.

---

## 📍 Method 3: Best Mining Locations

| Location | Why It's Great |
|----------|----------------|
| 🏞️ **Riverbank east of the van** | Dense rock clusters, zero travel time |
| 🏖️ **Coast cliff bases** | Large formations, great for bulk runs |
| ⛰️ **Open‑pit quarry near the lift** | Highest rock density on the map — one trip lasts a week |

---

## 📦 Supplemental: Loot from Crates

Breaking wooden crates in cabins, camps, and warehouses has a chance to drop **5–8 Stone**. A handy bonus while exploring — no need to detour.

---

## ⚠️ Rookie Errors to Avoid

- ❌ **Never use an axe on rocks** — it wastes durability and yields nothing.
- ❌ **Don't hoard stone early** — after your basic tools and campfire, demand drops sharply.
- ✅ Stone can serve as emergency smelter fuel, but wood and fiber are more efficient.

> 🧱 With these methods, you'll never be scrambling for stone again. Hit the riverbank early, then graduate to the quarry when you're ready for bulk building.
