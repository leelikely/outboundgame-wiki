# Outbound Beginner’s Guide – First 3 Days Walkthrough

Just starting your road trip? Follow this 3‑day guide to go from a stranded wanderer to a fully equipped van‑dweller.

---

## 🏁 Day 1 – Core Progression

1. **After exiting the tunnel**, collect scrap metal (found under street lights) and wood.
2. **Head to the first signal tower** and download the Wrench blueprint. Craft a wrench to open the fence.
3. **Build the Construction Hammer and Recycler**. Recycle scrap to earn Download Tickets (research points).
4. Unlock the **Axe and Sawmill** to solve wood shortages.
5. **Repair the broken bridge** (10 planks) to access the next area.

## ⚡ Early Priorities

- 🔋 **Power**: Use wood to charge your van early on; upgrade to solar panels later.
- 📦 **Storage**: Build extra storage boxes to avoid being over‑encumbered.
- 🌱 **Food**: Unlock Gardening and Food Processing; plant blueberries for a sustainable food supply.
- 🗺️ **Blueprints**: Visit every signal tower to download essential tools and structures.
