# Outbound Ultimate Van Customization Guide

Ready to build the perfect mobile base? Follow this upgrade priority, space‑planning advice, and energy management guide.

---

## 🔔 Upgrade Priority

1. ⚡ **Energy System**: Solar Panels > Wind Turbine > Hydro Generator (choose based on your biome).
2. 📦 **Space Expansion**: Rooftop Platform > Interior Partitions > External Storage.
3. 🏭 **Functional Areas**: Workbench > Kitchen > Farming Plot > Resting Area.
4. 🎨 **Decoration**: Save paint, furniture, and ornaments for last.

---

## 🧱 Space‑Planning Tips

- **Inside**: Use lower level for bedroom/storage, upper level for work and farming.
- **Roof**: Build an observation deck and place solar panels; use folding structures to save space.
- **Outside**: Attach roof racks and external toolboxes for extra carrying capacity.

---

## 🔌 Energy System Explained

- **Early game**: Burn wood and fiber to charge your battery (12 wood = full charge).
- **Mid game**: Install solar panels – free, passive charging during the day.
- **Late game**: Mix solar, wind, and water for total off‑grid independence.
