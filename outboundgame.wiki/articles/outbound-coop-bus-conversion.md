# The Hottest Trend in Outbound – Two‑Player Bus Conversion

Since the launch of *Outbound*, a new fan‑favorite playstyle has emerged: **two‑player school bus conversion**. Content creators are pushing the game’s building and social features to the limit, and the results are stunning.

---

## 🚐 Why the Bus?

The school bus is much larger than the starter van, offering:

- 📐 Multiple rooms to partition — bedroom, kitchen, lounge, and storage.
- 🪴 A roof deck for solar panels, gardens, or a chill‑out zone.
- 🔨 Dozens of customization options from basic framing to full‑on interior design.

But the bus is also a huge project. That’s where two‑player synergy shines.

---

## 👥 Perfect Duo Dynamics

In co‑op, one player can act as the **explorer**, driving to remote locations to gather rare materials, unlock blueprints, and tag signal towers. The other becomes the **builder**, staying at the campsite to plan the layout, craft furniture, and manage inventory.

This division of labor turns a daunting task into a smooth, enjoyable collaboration. Communication and creativity flow naturally.

---

## 🏆 The Ultimate Mobile Home

By the end, that dusty school bus is a fully functional, multi‑story home on wheels. Every plank placed brings a sense of achievement. There’s no rushing, no fighting — just two friends, a bus, and the open road.
