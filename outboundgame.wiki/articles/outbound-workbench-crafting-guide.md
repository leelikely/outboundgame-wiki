🔧 The Workbench is the heart of your crafting operation. It's available from the start, but its true power only unfolds as you unlock more blueprints. Here's everything you need to know.

---

## 🧰 Workbench Basics

The Workbench is built into the rear of your camper van — just open the back door and you'll see it.

1. Approach the Workbench and press `E` to open the crafting interface.
2. Browse the recipe list on the left.
3. Ensure you have the required materials (displayed below each recipe).
4. Click **"Craft"** — the finished item appears in your backpack instantly.

No power, no fuel, no cooldowns. It just works.

---

## 🔓 How to Unlock More Recipes

You start with only a handful of basic recipes. To expand your options, you need **Blueprints** from Signal Towers.

### Step‑by‑Step:
1. **Collect Litter** — small white bags scattered everywhere.
2. **Build a Recycler** — place it at the back of your van (free).
3. **Create Download Vouchers** — 2 Litter = 1 Voucher.
4. **Find a Signal Tower** — the first is just southeast of your spawn, at Fire Lookout.
5. **Download Blueprints** — interact with the computer beneath the tower, select a blueprint, and spend your vouchers.
6. **Recipes Unlocked** — they appear in your Workbench immediately and stay permanently.

## 🎯 Blueprint Priority (My Recommended Order)

1. 🥇 **Axe** — you can't progress without wood.
2. 🥈 **Sawmill** — turns logs into planks for bridge repairs.
3. 🥉 **Sickle** — triples your fiber yield.
4. 🏅 **Pickaxe** — unlocks stone and iron mining.
5. 🎖️ **Tanning Rack** — for leather production.

> 💡 **Multiplayer bonus**: If the host downloads a blueprint, **all players** in the session can use it. Coordinate with your squad to divide blueprint costs!
