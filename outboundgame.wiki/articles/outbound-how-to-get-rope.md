## 📜 Rope Crafting Recipe

| Ingredient | Quantity | Result |
|------------|----------|--------|
| 🌿 Fiber | 3 | 🪢 1 Rope |

- 🏠 **Crafted at**: Basic Workbench (van interior)
- 🔓 **Unlocked**: From the very start — no blueprint required

---

## 🌿 How to Farm Fiber (The Raw Material)

Since rope comes from fiber, efficient fiber farming is the real key.

### 🌱 Method A: Hand‑Pick Small Bushes (Early Game)
- Orange or green bushes scattered everywhere.
- Click to collect — **1 fiber per bush**.
- Slow but requires nothing.

### 🌾 Method B: Sickle on Large Bushes (3× Efficiency)
- Unlock the **Sickle** blueprint at the Fire Lookout (1 Download Voucher).
- Target the **tall, dense bushes** (much larger than the small ones).
- Each harvest yields **3 fiber** — triple the output.

### ♻️ Method C: Recycle Old Clothes (Emergency)
- Tattered shirts, trousers, and shoes from abandoned buildings.
- Drop into the Recycler → **1–2 fiber each**.

## 📍 Top Fiber Locations

| Location | Density |
|----------|---------|
| 🏞️ **Lakeshore &amp; riverbanks** — especially east of the van | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| 🔥 **South of Fire Lookout** — massive tall bush clusters | ⭐⭐⭐ |

> 🚐 Drive along the lake, hop out with your sickle, and you'll collect **50+ fiber in 10 minutes**.

## 🔧 What Rope Is Used For

- 🏹 Crafting bows and arrows
- 🪤 Building traps
- 🌉 Repairing bridges and paths
- 🎒 Backpack expansion upgrades
- 🏕️ Tents and shade structures
- 🪜 Ladders and climbing gear

---

## 🧠 Smart Rope Habits

- 📦 Rope stacks to **99** — craft in bulk and store it.
- 👥 In co‑op, assign one player as the dedicated fiber gatherer.
- 🔥 Fiber doubles as emergency smelter fuel — but only when you have surplus.

> 🪢 A few minutes of sickle farming along the lake will give you enough rope for days. Make it part of your routine and you'll never scramble again.
