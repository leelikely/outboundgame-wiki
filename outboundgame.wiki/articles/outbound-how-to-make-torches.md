🔥 Outbound's nights are **dark** — seriously dark. Without lighting, you're blind and vulnerable to creatures that roam after sunset. Torches are your first and most reliable defense against the dark.

---

## 📜 Torch Crafting Recipe

| Ingredient | Quantity | Result |
|------------|----------|--------|
| 🪵 Wood | 1 | 🔥 4 Torches |
| 🌿 Fiber | 1 | |

- 🏠 **Crafted at**: Basic Workbench (van interior)
- 🔓 **Unlocked**: Available immediately — no blueprint needed

---

## 🛠️ Step‑by‑Step Crafting

1. 🪓 Gather **1 Wood** (chop any tree) and **1 Fiber** (pick a bush).
2. 🚐 Return to your van and interact with the Workbench (`E`).
3. 📋 Select **"Torch"** from the recipe list.
4. 🔨 Click **"Craft"** — 4 torches appear in your backpack instantly.

---

## 🔦 How to Use Torches

### 🤲 Hand‑Held Mode
1. Drag torches from your backpack to the **hotbar** (keys 1–9).
2. Press the number key to equip — the torch ignites automatically.
3. ✅ **Pros**: Mobile lighting, great for exploring caves or night walks.
4. ❌ **Cons**: Occupies your hands — can't use tools or weapons simultaneously.

### 📍 Placed Mode
1. Equip a torch.
2. Aim at a **surface** (ground, wall, or ceiling).
3. 🖱️ **Right‑click** to place it.
4. 🔥 Placed torches burn **permanently** — they never go out.
5. ✅ **Pros**: Fixed illumination for your base, van perimeter, or cave paths.
6. ❌ **Cons**: Stationary — you'll need multiple torches to cover large areas.

---

## 💡 Strategic Torch Placement

| Location | Why |
|----------|-----|
| 🚐 **Around your van** | Prevents creatures from approaching at night |
| 🏠 **Inside your base** | Keeps crafting areas visible |
| 🕳️ **Cave interiors** | Place every 10–15 steps to light your path and **suppress monster spawns** |
| 🗺️ **Key path intersections** | Creates visual breadcrumbs so you never get lost |

## 🧠 Pro Tips

- 🧟 Most creatures **fear light** — they won't approach torch‑lit areas.
- 📦 Torches stack to **99** — craft a big batch and never worry about darkness again.
- 🧭 If you're lost at night, head toward **any light source** — it's probably your van or a torch you placed earlier.
- 🕳️ In caves, place torches frequently — they prevent enemies from spawning behind you.

> 🌙 Night exploration is one of Outbound's greatest joys — but only if you're prepared. Stock up on torches, light your path, and the darkness becomes an adventure instead of a threat.
