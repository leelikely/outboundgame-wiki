📦 Need planks early game but haven’t built the Sawmill? These four methods will keep you supplied.

## 🏚️ 1. Scavenge the Ground
Explore abandoned houses, warehouses, and roadside ruins. Planks often lie in piles – simply walk up and collect **1–2 at a time**.

## 📦 2. Smash Wooden Crates
Crates are everywhere. Use your axe (or even fists) to break them. There’s a high chance of obtaining planks, sometimes even **Everwood Planks**.

## 🪑 3. Dismantle Furniture
In derelict buildings, break tables, chairs, and cupboards with your axe. Each gives **1–3 planks**. Be careful – the noise may attract nearby enemies, so clear the area first.

## 🤝 4. Trading (Late Game)
Certain NPCs will exchange planks for other materials, but the rates are poor. Only use this if you have an extreme surplus.

## 📊 Summary
| Method | Effort | Yield |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Scavenging | Low | Low |
| Smashing crates | Low | Medium |
| Furniture recycling | Medium | High |
| Trading | High | Low |

> 🪓 **Top tip**: Make it a habit to break every crate you see. Over a few hours, you’ll accumulate more planks than you expect.
