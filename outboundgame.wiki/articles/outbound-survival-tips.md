# Outbound Survival & Exploration Tips

Go beyond the basics with these advanced survival and exploration tips.

---

## 🪓 Efficient Resource Gathering

- 🌲 **Wood**: Head to large forest zones; one big tree yields 5 wood.
- 🗑️ **Junk**: Each campsite has fixed spawn points; light the campfire to mark it as visited.
- 🎟️ **Download Tickets**: Build multiple Recyclers and process junk in bulk to save travel time.

---

## 🌱 Farming Guide

- **Best crop**: Blueberries (1 seed yields 3 berries, highest return).
- **Smart watering**: Place planting boxes on the roof; rain will water them automatically.
- **Advanced**: Unlock the Greenhouse to grow mushrooms and rare plants.

---

## 🐕 Pet System

- **How to get**: Visit the Cat & Dog Cabin (coordinates: 2200, 500). Build a pet house and offer food.
- **Benefits**: Pets accompany you, help locate hidden resources, and alert you to weather changes.
- **Training**: Feed and pet them to increase affection and unlock skills.

---

## 👥 Multiplayer Tips

- 🤝 **Divide tasks**: One player explores, one builds – double efficiency.
- 📤 **Shared inventory**: All storage in the van and crates is accessible to every teammate.
- 🔧 **Co‑op puzzles**: Repairing bridges or clearing obstacles requires teamwork.
