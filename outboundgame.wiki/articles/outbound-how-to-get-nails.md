🔩 Nails are one of those materials you never think about — until you can't build anything without them. From basic shelving to reinforced van walls, nails underpin most of your construction. Here's every way to get them.

---

## 🏭 Method 1: Crafting (Most Reliable)

Once you've entered the Iron Age, you can manufacture nails yourself.

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| 📜 **Recipe** | **1 Iron Ingot → 4 Nails** |
| 🔥 **Crafted at** | Metal Furnace (Smelter) |
| 🔓 **Unlocked** | Automatically with the Furnace blueprint |

**Steps:**
1. ⛏️ Mine iron ore and smelt it into Iron Ingots (see my Iron Guide if needed).
2. 🏠 Return to your van and interact with the Furnace (`E`).
3. 📋 Select **"Nails"** from the recipe list.
4. 🔨 Click **"Craft"** — 4 nails appear in your backpack instantly.

---

## 🏚️ Method 2: Scavenging (Early Game)

Before you have a Furnace, scavenge these locations:

- 🏠 **Abandoned houses and warehouses** — check floors and corners
- 🏗️ **Construction sites** — especially rich in nails
- 📦 **Wooden crates and barrels** — smash them for a chance at **1–2 nails**

🔥 **Hot spot:** The barn at **Sunbeam Acres Farm** has a high nail density — I found over a dozen there in one run.

---

## 🪓 Method 3: Dismantle Furniture (Supplemental)

Break furniture with your axe while exploring ruins:

| Furniture Type | Nail Yield |
|----------------|------------|
| 🪑 Wooden tables & chairs | Small chance of **1 nail** |
| 🛏️ Metal furniture | Good chance of **2–3 nails** |

---

## 🛠️ What Nails Are Used For

- 📦 Storage shelves and lockers
- 🧱 Reinforced van walls and floors
- 🪑 Advanced furniture crafting
- 🏗️ House and structure construction
- 🔧 Certain tools and weapons

---

## 🧠 Smart Nail Habits

- 📦 Nails stack to **99** — craft a big batch and store the excess.
- ⏳ Don't stress early on — pre‑Iron Age building demands very few nails.
- 🏭 Once your iron supply is stable, craft **40–50 nails in one go** — you'll be set for days.
- 👥 In co‑op, assign one player as the dedicated nail crafter.

> 🔩 With a steady iron income, nails become trivial. Focus on getting that Furnace up, and you'll never scramble again.
