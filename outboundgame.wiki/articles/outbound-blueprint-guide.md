# Outbound Blueprint Locations – Every Signal Tower & Hidden Recipe

Unlocking blueprints is the key to progression. This guide covers all known signal tower rewards and hidden recipes.

---

## 🗼 Signal Tower Blueprints (in unlock order)

| Location | Blueprints | Tickets |
|----------|------------|---------|
| Starting area | Wrench, Construction Hammer, Recycler | 1-2 |
| First Watchtower | Axe, Sickle, Pickaxe | 2-3 |
| Left side of broken bridge | Sawmill, Gardening, Food Processor | 3-4 |
| Right side of broken bridge | Rooftop Platform, Furniture Set | 4-5 |
| Forest region | Solar Panel, Wind Turbine, Watering Can | 5-6 |
| Coast region | Hydro Generator, Greenhouse, Pet House | 6-7 |

---

## 🔍 Hidden Blueprints

- 🎣 **Fishing Rod**: Found at the Lakeside Camp, where a small fishing figure sits.
- 🧵 **Sewing Machine**: Unlocked via terminal in the Treehouse area (full release).
- 🐾 **Pet Training Manual**: Complete the quest at the Cat & Dog Cabin.
