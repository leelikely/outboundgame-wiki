# Outbound – The Ultimate Relaxing Game for Stress Relief

In today’s fast‑paced world, finding a game that truly helps you unwind is rare. *Outbound* (拾光旅人) is exactly that — a gentle, healing escape from reality.

---

## 🌿 No Combat, No Rankings, No Rush

Unlike most survival games, *Outbound* has **no hunger bars, no enemies, and no forced progression**. You simply exist. You drive. You build. You relax.

- 🛤️ Cruise through lush forests, meadows, and coastline.
- 🎨 Customize your van’s paint, furniture, and layout at your leisure.
- 🏞️ Stop anywhere, set up camp, and enjoy the view — no need to rush to the next objective.

---

## 👥 Share the Calm with Friends

Play solo for a meditative experience, or invite up to three friends. Build a campsite together, cook meals, and watch the stars. It’s a game that encourages conversation and laughter, not competition.

If you’re an overworked professional, a stressed student, or anyone who needs a digital oasis, *Outbound* is exactly what you’ve been looking for.
