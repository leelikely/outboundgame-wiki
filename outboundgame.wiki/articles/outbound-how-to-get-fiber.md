Fiber is essential for crafting ropes, sofas, and other items. Here’s how to farm it efficiently.

## 🌿 Basic Method (no tools):

1. Look for small orange or green bushes on the map.
2. Walk up to a bush and press the left mouse button.
3. Each harvest gives **1 fiber**.

## 🌾 Efficient Method (with Sickle):

1. Unlock the **Sickle** blueprint at a Signal Tower (1 Download Voucher).
2. Craft the Sickle at your van's workbench and equip it.
3. Target large bushes (much taller than the small ones).
4. Hold down the left mouse button – each harvest yields **3 fiber**.

## 📍 Best Farming Locations:

- Lakeshores and riverbanks (abundant orange bushes)
- North of the Fire Lookout
- Around The Community Tree
