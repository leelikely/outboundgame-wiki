---
title: How to Use the Axe in Outbound
date: 2026-05-12
category: Guides
excerpt: Step‑by‑step instructions to equip and use the axe to chop trees and gather wood in Outbound.
---

**Prerequisite:** You have already unlocked and crafted the Axe.

1. Press `Tab` to open your backpack.
2. Drag the Axe to any slot on the hotbar (keys 1‑9).
3. Press the corresponding number key to equip the axe.
4. Look for dead trees or regular trees on the map.
5. Approach the tree until you see the **“Chop”** prompt.
6. Hold down the left mouse button to chop.
7. Once the tree falls, collect the dropped logs.
8. ⚠️ The axe has durability – craft a new one when it breaks.
