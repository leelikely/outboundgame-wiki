## 🔍 Tips for Finding Chests

| Tip | Why It Helps |
|-----|--------------|
| 🔎 **Check behind buildings** | Chests are often hidden behind structures |
| 👂 **Listen for the chime** | Chests emit a faint ringing sound when you're close |
| 🗺️ **Watch your map** | Some chests appear as tiny chest icons |
| 🏔️ **Climb high** | Elevated vantage points reveal hidden corners |
| 🔄 **Revisit after sleeping** | Sleep resets respawn timers — ideal for farming |

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- 🎲 Chest contents are **randomised** each respawn — no two openings are identical.
- 📜 **Rare chest blueprints** are unique and can't be obtained elsewhere — prioritise these.
- 👥 In co‑op, chests are **shared** — first come, first served. Communicate with your team!
- 🔁 If a chest seems to be missing, try reloading the game — spawn positions can shift slightly.

> 📦 Happy hunting! The thrill of discovering a golden chest tucked behind a waterfall or perched on a cliff edge is one of Outbound's greatest joys.
