🧵 Fiber is vital for ropes, cloth, and bandages. Here are three ways to gather it, from early‑game to bulk farming.

## 🌿 Method 1: Hand‑picking Small Bushes (Beginners)
Walk up to orange or green bushes and press left mouse button. Each bush gives **1 fiber**. It’s slow but requires no tools.

## 🌾 Method 2: Sickle on Large Bushes (Best Yield)
- Unlock the **Sickle** blueprint at a Signal Tower (1 Download Voucher).
- Craft and equip it.
- Search for **tall, leafy bushes** — much larger than the small ones.
- Holding the mouse button yields **3 fiber per bush**, triple the normal rate.

## ♻️ Method 3: Recycle Old Clothes (Emergency)
Found a torn shirt or broken shoes? Put them into the Recycler. Each clothing item gives **1–2 fiber**. Useful when you’re stuck without bushes.

## 📍 Best Farming Spots
- Lakeshores and riverbanks (tons of orange bushes)
- North of the Fire Lookout
- Around The Community Tree

> 🚐 **Pro tip**: Drive along a riverbank, hopping out to sickle bushes. Ten minutes can net you enough fiber for days.
