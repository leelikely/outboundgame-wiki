🤔 So you made an axe — but why won’t it work? Almost every beginner tries to place it in the **equipment slot** and gets confused.

## ❌ The Common Mistake
The axe is a **tool**, not clothing. Dragging it to your character’s outfit panel won’t do anything.

## ✅ How to Properly Equip It
1. Press `Tab` to open your backpack.
2. Go to the **“Items”** tab.
3. Drag the axe **down to the hotbar** (any slot from 1 to 9).
4. Release the mouse button.
5. Press the matching number key to equip it.

Your character will now hold the axe, ready to chop.

## 🔁 This Works for Other Tools Too
The same method applies to the Sickle, Hammer, Pickaxe, and any future tools you craft. Keep your most‑used tools on the hotbar for quick switching.

> 🧰 **My setup**: Slot 1 – Axe, Slot 2 – Sickle, Slot 3 – Hammer. This muscle memory speeds up all my gathering runs.
