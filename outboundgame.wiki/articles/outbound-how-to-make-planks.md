---
title: How to Make Planks in Outbound
date: 2026-05-12
category: Guides
excerpt: Step‑by‑step guide to crafting regular planks from logs at the Sawmill.
---

**Prerequisites:** Sawmill blueprint unlocked and Sawmill built inside your van.

1. Gather **Regular Wood Logs** from regular trees or by picking them up in forested areas.
2. Return to your van and stand next to the Sawmill.
3. Press `E` to open the Sawmill interface.
4. Drag Regular Wood Logs into the left input slot.
5. Select **“Plank”** from the recipe list on the right.
6. Click the **“Craft”** button.
7. 📐 Conversion ratio: **2 Regular Logs → 1 Plank**.
