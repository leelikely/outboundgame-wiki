# Demo Version 1.0.0.6

**Release Date:** February 24, 2026

## Change Notes

- Reduced the frequency of Voice Over lines, but also added a setting to increase the frequency again
- Added more variety for choosing skin tones in character customization
- Fixed respawning in fire, which could cause an endless fainting loop
- Increased visibility of the "Multiplayer is unlocked" messaging
- Various translation improvements
