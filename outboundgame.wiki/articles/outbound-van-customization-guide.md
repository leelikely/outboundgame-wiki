# Outbound Van Customization – Let Your Imagination Run Wild

Among the wave of cozy multiplayer games, *Outbound* stands out with its unique road‑trip camping gameplay. The game fully supports four‑player online co‑op, making it the perfect choice for hanging out with friends.

No stressful combat or grinding — just you, the open road, and a customizable camper van. Invite your friends, pick a direction, and start exploring a vast open world. Stop wherever you like, set up camp, and enjoy the scenery.

---

## 🔧 Vehicle Customization: The Heart of Outbound

Van customization is where the game truly shines. You can:

- 🧱 **Gather building materials** from the environment — scrap, wood, fabric, and more.
- 🎨 **Repaint the exterior** and design the interior layout.
- 🛋️ **Place furniture, storage, crafting stations** exactly where you want them.
- 🚐 **Transform a basic van into a cozy mobile home** that reflects your personal style.

No two vans ever look the same. Whether you prefer a minimalist workshop or a fully decorated living space, the tools are in your hands.

---

## 🧘‍♂️ No Pressure, All Pleasure

There are no mandatory quests, no combat rankings, no deadlines. *Outbound* is all about slow, heal­ing gameplay. You set the pace. You decide what to build next.

Whether you’re a casual explorer, a dedicated builder, or just want to chat with friends while roaming the wilder­ness, this game delivers a truly relaxing experience.
