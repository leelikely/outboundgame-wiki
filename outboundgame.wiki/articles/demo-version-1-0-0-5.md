# Demo Version 1.0.0.5

**Release Date:** February 19, 2026

## Change Notes

Because we saw Raptor trying something cool in his stream on Tuesday – covering a campfire with the side door while it's raining – we decided that should indeed work!  
🔥 You can now **cover a campfire with the horizontal doors of your vehicle** or with your **base building floors and roofs**.

- Fix on updating material counts on tasks if another player puts materials into the vehicle
- Improvements to the building UI
