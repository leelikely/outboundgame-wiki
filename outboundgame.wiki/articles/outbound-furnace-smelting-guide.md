🔥 The Furnace (Smelter) is one of the most important crafting stations in Outbound. Without it, you're stuck in the Stone Age — no iron tools, no reinforced buildings, no advanced weapons. Here's your complete guide.

---

## 🏗️ How to Craft the Furnace

### Step 1: Unlock the Blueprint
- 🗼 Visit **The Large Treehouse** Signal Tower
- 💳 Spend **2 Download Vouchers** on the Furnace blueprint

### Step 2: Gather Materials

| Material | Quantity |
|----------|----------|
| 🪨 Stone | 8 |
| 🪚 Planks | 4 |
| 🪢 Rope | 2 |

### Step 3: Build It
1. 🚐 Return to your van and press `B` to enter build mode.
2. 🔍 Find **"Furnace"** under the Processing category.
3. 📍 Place it inside your van — ideally near the Workbench.
4. ✅ Confirm — the Furnace is ready to use.

---

## ⚙️ How to Use the Furnace

1. Walk up to the Furnace and press `E`.
2. ⬆️ Place raw ore in the **top input slot**.
3. ⬇️ Add **fuel** to the bottom slot.
4. ▶️ Click **"Start Smelting"**.
5. ⏱️ Wait for the progress bar — finished ingots appear in the output slot.

---

## 📊 Smelting Ratios & Times

| Raw Material | Product | Ratio | Smelt Time |
|--------------|---------|-------|-------------|
| ⛏️ Iron Ore | 🪨 Iron Ingot | 2:1 | ~5 sec |
| 🟤 Copper Ore | 🟠 Copper Ingot | 2:1 | ~5 sec |
| ✨ Gold Ore | 🟡 Gold Ingot | 3:1 | ~10 sec |

---

## 🔥 Fuel Comparison

| Fuel | Burn Time | Items Smelted | Ease of Obtaining |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| 🌿 Fiber | 10 sec | 2 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (easiest) |
| 🪵 Wood | 30 sec | 6 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 🪨 Coal | 60 sec | 12 | ⭐⭐⭐ |

> 🪵 **My recommendation:** Use wood as your primary fuel. It burns long enough for most jobs and is trivially easy to farm. Fiber is fine in emergencies; coal is best saved for bulk smelting sessions.

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- 🏭 The Furnace can process **20+ ore at once** — load it up, walk away, and come back to a pile of ingots.
- 🔌 No power required — only fuel.
- 📦 Place a storage crate next to the Furnace so you can dump finished ingots without cluttering your backpack.
- 👥 In co‑op, split roles: one person mines, the other smelts.

> ⚙️ Once your Furnace is running, the entire mid‑game unlocks. Prioritise this build — it's a game‑changer.
