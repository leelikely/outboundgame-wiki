🗺️ As of the latest v0.8.3 update (June 2026), Outbound features **three complete explorable regions**, with a fourth in development. Here's your comprehensive tour.

---

## 🌲 Region 1: The Outdoors

Your birthplace and the largest, most content‑rich zone: forests, meadows, wheat fields, and mountains.

| Landmark | Coordinates / Location | Key Rewards |
|----------|------------------------|-------------|
| 🔥 **Fire Lookout** | Highest hill in starting meadow | Axe, Sickle, Pickaxe blueprints |
| 🌲 **The Large Treehouse** | North of Fire Lookout | Metal Smelter, Nail Gun blueprints |
| 🌬️ **Lilly's Windmill** | Northwest of Treehouse | Mortar &amp; Pestle (repair required) |
| 🌻 **Sunbeam Acres Farm** | Far east | Wheat, carrots, locked farmhouse |
| 🏚️ **Woods Cabin** | Deep forest | Survival tools |
| 🏔️ **Mountain Outpost** | Northern hills | Storage crates, rare loot |

| Landmark | Highlights |

## 🧭 Navigation &amp; Exploration Tips

- 🗺️ Press `M` to view the map — explored areas are revealed; grey zones are unexplored.
- 📍 Click anywhere to set a waypoint.
- 🔖 Each Signal Tower and landmark has a unique icon.
- 📓 Open your Journal (`Tab` → Journal) for a list of discovered locations and collectibles.

> 🌄 Outbound's world is hand‑crafted with care. Take your time, explore every corner, and you'll be rewarded with secrets most players never find.
