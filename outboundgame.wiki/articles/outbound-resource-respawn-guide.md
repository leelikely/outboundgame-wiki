🔄 *"I cleared this area — will it ever come back?"* Yes! Almost every resource in Outbound respawns. Knowing the timers lets you build efficient farming loops and avoid wasted trips. Here's everything I've learned after dozens of hours of play.

---

## 📏 Basic Respawn Rules

Two conditions must be met for any resource to respawn:

1. 📍 **Distance**: You must leave the area (move far enough away).
2. ⏱️ **Time**: A specific cooldown must pass before the resource reappears.

If you stand in one spot, nothing refreshes. Keep moving.

---

## ⏳ Respawn Timers (Complete Table)

| Resource Type | Respawn Time | Notes |
|---------------|--------------|-------|
| 🌿 Fiber (bushes) | 5–10 min | Fastest refresh — great for loop farming |
| 🗑️ Litter | 10–15 min | Even faster near Signal Towers |
| 🪨 Stone (large rocks) | 15–20 min | Fixed spawn points |
| ⛏️ Iron Ore | 20–30 min | Fixed spawn points |
| 🌳 Dead Trees (Everwood) | 30–45 min | Slower — plan your logging routes |
| 📦 Standard Chests | 1–2 hours | Contains basic materials and tools |
| ✨ Rare Chests | 6–12 hours | Contains rare materials and blueprints |

---

## 🧪 Factors That Affect Respawns

| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| 📏 **Distance from area** | Must be far enough away for the timer to start |
| ⏰ **Time elapsed** | Real‑world time must pass |
| 🛌 **Sleeping** | Instantly resets **all** respawn timers |
| 👥 **Multiplayer** | May be slightly affected by server load |
| 📦 **Game version** | Developers occasionally adjust timers in updates |

---

## 🗺️ Efficient Farming Strategies

### 🔁 Loop Farming
Don't camp one spot. Create a **circular route** through 3–4 resource‑rich zones. By the time you complete the loop, the first zone has respawned.

### 🛌 Sleep‑Reset Method
Clear every resource in an area, then go to sleep. When you wake up, everything has respawned — including trees, ore, and chests. This is the single most efficient farming technique in the game.

### 📍 Map Markers
Pin high‑density resource zones on your map. Revisit them on a schedule:
- 🌿 Fiber spots → every 10 minutes
- ⛏️ Iron spots → every 30 minutes
- 📦 Chests → every 2 hours

### 👥 Co‑op Splitting
Assign each player a different zone. Reconvene every 15–20 minutes to pool resources — quadruple efficiency.

---

## ❌ Resources That NEVER Respawn

These are one‑time pickups — treat them with care:

- 📜 Quest items
- 🏆 Certain rare collectibles
- 📖 One‑off story items

> 🔄 **Stuck waiting for a resource?** Try quitting to the main menu and reloading — this occasionally forces a refresh on stubborn nodes.
