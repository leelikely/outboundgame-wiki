📈 Many players focus on gathering and crafting without realising Outbound has a leveling system that unlocks build slots, backpack capacity, and hidden recipe permissions. To progress smoothly, you need to know which actions yield the most experience. I've ranked every stable XP source in the current version by efficiency — follow this guide and your leveling speed will double.

## 🎯 What Does Leveling Actually Do?

Each level up brings tangible, permanent bonuses:

| Level Reward | Benefit |
|--------------|---------|
| 🎒 **Backpack capacity** | Higher max stack sizes |
| 🏗️ **Build slots** | More van expansion room |
| 🔧 **Tool durability** | Tools last longer |
| 📜 **Blueprint access** | Unlocks certain advanced recipes |
| ❤️ **Base HP & stamina** | Small increases to both pools |

Even just 3–5 levels early game dramatically smooths the experience.

## 📊 XP Efficiency Tier List

I've grouped all activities into three tiers based on XP per minute.

### 🥇 T0 Tier: Blueprint Unlocks & Landmark Repairs *(Highest single payouts)*

This is the fastest XP in the game — nothing else comes close.

- 🗼 **Activating a new Signal Tower** — massive XP boost
- 📜 **Downloading a new blueprint** — fixed XP per blueprint
- 🌉 **Repairing bridges, windmills, lighthouses** — enormous XP, often enough to jump multiple levels
- 🧭 **Discovering new regions** — exploration XP bonus

> 💡 **Pro tip:** Pushing the main story is by far the fastest way to level. Gather enough Download Vouchers and unlock Signal Towers — the XP will flow naturally.

### 🥈 T1 Tier: High‑Value Gathering & Advanced Crafting

When you're stuck and can't unlock new areas yet, farm these:

| Activity | XP Notes |
|----------|----------|
| ⛏️ **Mining ore (especially Iron)** | Far more XP per node than chopping trees |
| 🔥 **Smelting ore into ingots** | Bonus XP for each conversion |
| 🪚 **Crafting planks, leather, etc.** | Processing raw materials always pays |
| 🔨 **Crafting tools & equipment** | Iron tools give especially good XP |
| 🐻 **Hunting elite monsters** | High XP + valuable loot drops |

📦 **Don't hoard raw materials.** Turn wood into planks, ore into ingots, and hide into leather — you'll earn both materials and XP simultaneously.

### 🥉 T2 Tier: Basic Gathering & Daily Tasks

These give XP but are inefficient to grind intentionally:

- 🌲 Chopping regular trees
- 🌿 Picking fiber
- 🗑️ Collecting litter
- 📦 Crafting basic items
- 🐺 Killing small wildlife
- 📍 Discovering common landmarks

Do these while traveling, not as a dedicated XP grind.

## 🚀 Fastest Leveling Route (Levels 0–5)

Follow this route to reach Level 5 in under two hours:

1. 🔍 Gather litter around your van → craft **5 Download Vouchers**
2. 🗼 Head to the first Signal Tower → download **Axe, Pickaxe, Sickle** blueprints (big XP spike)
3. ⛏️ Craft the Pickaxe → mine stone and iron ore (steady XP while gathering)
4. 🔥 Unlock Sawmill & Furnace → process all your raw materials
5. 🧱 Collect **10 Everwood Planks** → repair the Northern Bridge → enter the Coast (huge XP jump)

## ❌ Common Leveling Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Slows You Down |
|---------|------------------------|
| 🔄 Repeatedly crafting low‑tier items | Planks and rope give tiny XP — craft iron tools instead |
| 📦 Hoarding raw materials | Processed materials give far more XP |
| 🏕️ Staying in one zone | No exploration = no discovery or unlock rewards |
| 🐺 Farming small mobs | Wolves and crabs are worth almost no XP |

## 👥 Multiplayer XP Tips

XP is **shared** in co‑op! When a teammate unlocks a blueprint or repairs a landmark, everyone gains experience. Split roles: one player rushes Signal Towers while others gather and craft at home — the whole squad levels up rapidly.
