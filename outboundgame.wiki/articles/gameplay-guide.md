# Gameplay Guide

In Outbound, stress takes a backseat. There are no fail‑states, no forced questlines, and no hostile mobs — just a world begging to be explored, a van begging to be upgraded, and a lifestyle begging to be embraced.

---

## 🚐 Your Camper Van: More Than a Vehicle

Your van is your base, your storage, and your creative playground. Using the modular building system, you can:

- **Expand the interior** by snapping together walls, floors, and furniture.
- **Customize the look** with paints, lighting, and decorations.
- **Build workbenches** to craft components, upgrade tools, and unlock new recipes.
- **Install rooftop structures** and even set up a campsite beside the van using the “Base Building Deck.”

The only limit is your imagination — and the handful of rare materials you’ll hunt down in the wild.

---

## 🌾 Sustainable Living

Outbound celebrates self‑sufficiency. You’ll grow crops, cook meals, and process raw materials into advanced goods. Energy comes from the sun, wind, or rushing water. The goal isn’t just survival; it’s building a circular ecosystem that hums along while you watch the sunset.

---

## 🏞️ A World of Discovery

The map is split into distinctive regions, such as **The Outdoors** and **The Coast**, each with its own biome, wildlife, and secrets:

- Friendly and skittish animals roam freely.
- Hidden landmarks and environmental puzzles reward curiosity.
- Weather changes dynamically — rain, thunder, clear skies — but never punishes. You can always close the van door and cook indoors.

---

## 👥 Co‑op Multiplayer

Invite up to three friends. Your van becomes a **shared mobile inventory**:

- Materials dropped in the van are instantly available for everyone at any crafting station.
- Divide tasks: one player drives, another builds, a third prepares meals.
- Progress syncs seamlessly, making collaboration feel natural and rewarding.

---

## 🧘 Your Own Pace

There are no mandatory missions. Instead, you’ll find gentle **side tasks** that teach you mechanics (like locking materials in your backpack, or crafting the Base Building Deck). You set your goals, your schedule, and your definition of a perfect day.
