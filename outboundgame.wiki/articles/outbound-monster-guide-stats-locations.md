🐺 Many newcomers treat Outbound as pure cozy camping — until something attacks them in the dark. While the current version doesn't have dozens of enemies, each species has distinct spawn zones and attack patterns. Learn them, and you'll walk away from every fight unscathed.

## 🐺 Forest Wolf

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| ❤️ **HP** | ~25 |
| ⚔️ **Damage** | 8–10 per bite |
| 🗡️ **Attacks** | Melee bite; telegraphs with a low head‑dip before pouncing |
| 📍 **Spawns** | Forests and riverbanks in the starting region (day & night) |

### 🎯 Strategy

Wolves are fast and like to flank. Backpedaling won't work — they'll just chase you down.

- 🛡️ **Don't run away** — their speed outstrips yours unarmed.
- ⏱️ Wait for the **head‑dip lunge**, then **sidestep** and counter with 2–3 swings.
- 🏹 A bow makes wolves trivial — they can't close the gap.
- 👥 If you face a pack of two or more, **run to create distance**, then pick them off one by one.

📦 **Drops:** Hide, Raw Meat — essential for leather and cooking.

## 🐗 Wild Boar

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| ❤️ **HP** | ~40 |
| ⚔️ **Damage** | 15 (charge) / 10 (close shove) |
| 🗡️ **Attacks** | Long‑range charge; close‑range head shove |
| 📍 **Spawns** | Sunbeam Acres Farm, wheat fields, open grasslands (daytime active) |

### 🎯 Strategy

The charge is the real threat — it can knock off a third of your health and interrupt your actions.

- 👀 When it lowers its head and paws the ground, **dodge sideways**, not backward.
- 💥 The boar has a short recovery after a missed charge — exploit this window for 3–4 hits.
- 🪓 Melee is more efficient than a bow — its HP pool is too large for cheap arrows.
- 🚫 Avoid fighting in narrow spaces — no room to dodge means repeated knockbacks.

📦 **Drops:** Large amounts of Raw Meat, Hide.

## 👻 Night Wanderer

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| ❤️ **HP** | ~35 |
| ⚔️ **Damage** | 12 per hit |
| 🗡️ **Attacks** | Silent approach; melee swipe with long wind‑up |
| 📍 **Spawns** | Everywhere at night — more common in dark areas |

### 🎯 Strategy

The real danger is surprise. In pitch darkness, you won't see it coming.

- 🔥 Stay near **light sources** (torches, campfires, van) — this drastically reduces spawns.
- 👂 Listen for a low **growl** behind you — turn immediately.
- ⚔️ Its attack animation is slow; stand your ground, land two hits, backstep, repeat.
- 🛏️ Honestly? Just **sleep through the night**. Night Wanderers drop low‑value loot.

📦 **Drops:** Scrap cloth, decayed fragments (barely worth the fight).

## 🐻 Forest Bear *(Elite)*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| ❤️ **HP** | ~80 |
| ⚔️ **Damage** | 20 (swipe) / 30 (heavy slam) |
| 🗡️ **Attacks** | Heavy paw swipe, wide‑area slam; enrages at half HP |
| 📍 **Spawns** | Northern mountains, deep woods near the Large Treehouse (rare spawn) |

### 🎯 Strategy

This is the strongest enemy in the starting region. Do not engage without iron weapons.

- 🔪 Bring an **Iron Axe or Bow** — stone tools take forever and leave no margin for error.
- 📏 Keep **medium distance**: bait the swipe → backstep → counterattack during recovery.
- ⚡ At 50% HP, it attacks faster — don't get greedy; one hit and retreat.
- 👥 In co‑op, one player draws aggro while the other deals damage — it melts quickly.

📦 **Drops:** Large amounts of Hide, Prime Meat — essential for advanced backpacks and hiking boots.

## 🦀 Coastal Crab

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| ❤️ **HP** | ~20 |
| ⚔️ **Damage** | 8 per pinch |
| 🗡️ **Attacks** | Dual pincer strike; retreats into shell for damage reduction |
| 📍 **Spawns** | Beaches and rocky shores of the Coast region |

### 🎯 Strategy

Looks intimidating, but it's an XP piñata.

- 🛡️ **Never attack the front** — its shell blocks most damage.
- 🔄 Circle around and strike its **flanks and rear**.
- 🐚 When it hides in its shell, stop swinging — wait for it to emerge.
- 🗡️ Any weapon works; focus on positioning over gear.

📦 **Drops:** Crab meat, Shell (used in certain armor pieces).

## ⚔️ General Battle Tips

| Tip | Why It Helps |
|-----|--------------|
| 🔥 **All creatures fear fire** | Holding a torch causes many enemies to back away |
| 🏃 **Don't fight near resource nodes** | Kill monsters first, then gather in peace |
| 🚐 **Run to your van** | Enemies can't enter your camper — use it as a safe haven |
| 🔨 **Upgrade your weapon first** | A single Iron Axe is enough to comfortably handle every common foe |
