🥾 Hiking Boots are one of the most impactful equipment pieces in Outbound. They enhance mobility significantly, but you need to handle them correctly.

## 🔧 How to Obtain Hiking Boots
1. Unlock the blueprint at the **second Signal Tower** (costs 2 Download Vouchers).
2. Craft them at your van using **5 Fiber** and **3 Leather**. (Leather can be obtained by hunting animals.)

## 👟 Equipping the Boots
1. Press `Tab` to open inventory.
2. Click the **“Clothing”** tab.
3. Locate the Hiking Boots and drag them to the **“Feet”** slot on the right‑side equipment panel.
4. Release — they are now active.

## 📈 Bonuses (Passive)
- 🏃 **+15% movement speed**
- ⛰️ Reduced stamina drain on rough and mountainous terrain
- 🧗 Improved ability to climb steep slopes

## ⚠️ Durability Warning
Hiking Boots have limited durability. When they wear out, they vanish, and you’ll need to craft a new pair. Before heading into dangerous zones, **check the durability bar** on the item.

> 🌲 **Pro tip**: Combine Hiking Boots with a fast vehicle engine for the ultimate road‑trip pace.
