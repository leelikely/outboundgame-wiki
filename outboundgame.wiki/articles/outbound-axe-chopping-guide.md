🪓 The axe is your most essential tool in Outbound. But many beginners waste durability because they don’t know the basics.

## 🌳 Which Trees Can You Chop?
- ✅ **Dead trees** (grey trunks) — drop **Everwood Logs**
- ✅ **Regular trees** (brown trunks) — drop **Regular Wood Logs**
- ❌ **Living Everwood trees** (dark green) — cannot be chopped

## 🎮 How to Chop
1. Place the axe on your hotbar (number keys 1‑9).
2. Equip it by pressing the corresponding number.
3. Walk up to a valid tree until the **“Chop”** prompt appears.
4. Hold down the left mouse button to swing.
5. After the tree falls, collect the logs lying on the ground.

Each swing uses 1 durability; a standard axe lasts about **10 trees**.

## 💡 Pro Tips
- Stand **to the side** of the tree — when it falls, logs will land in a tight pile, saving collection time.
- Don’t stand in the fall direction — you’ll take minor damage.
- You can also break wooden crates and barrels with the axe to find rare materials.
