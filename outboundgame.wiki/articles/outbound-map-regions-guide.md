🗺️ Outbound's open world is vast, beautiful, and easy to get lost in. This guide covers every major region, landmark, and point of interest available as of the latest 2026 build.

---

## 🌲 Region 1: The Outdoors

Your starting area — a sprawling mix of dense forests, open meadows, golden wheat fields, and mountain ranges.

### 🔑 Key Landmarks

| Landmark | Location | What You'll Find |
|----------|----------|------------------|
| 🔥 **Fire Lookout** | Highest hill in the starting meadow | Axe, Sickle, Pickaxe blueprints |
| 🌲 **The Large Treehouse** | North of Fire Lookout | Metal Smelter, Nail Gun blueprints |
| 🌬️ **Lilly's Windmill** | Northwest of the Treehouse | Mortar &amp; Pestle blueprint (requires repair) |
| 🌻 **Sunbeam Acres Farm** | Far east of the map | Wheat fields, carrots, locked farmhouse |
| 🏚️ **Woods Cabin** | Deep forest | Survival tools, supplies |
| 🏔️ **Mountain Outpost** | Northern hills | Storage crates, rare materials |

## 🏖️ Region 2: The Coast

Accessible after repairing the northern bridge with 10 Everwood Planks. Features tropical beaches, rocky cliffs, redwood groves, and palm trees.

### 🔑 Key Landmarks

| Landmark | Highlights |
|----------|------------|
| 🐾 **Paws &amp; Whiskers Lodge** | Adopt a pet dog companion! |
| 🗼 **The Lighthouse** | Iconic coastal landmark; requires an environmental puzzle to restore |
| ☕ **The Coffee Farm** | Abandoned plantation with coffee beans and rare resources |
| 🏘️ **Heartmoor Float Village** | Floating village built on a lake — unique advanced building blueprints |
| 🌉 **Stormhold Overpass** | Derelict highway bridge packed with loot and blueprints |

## 🧭 Map Navigation Tips

- Press `M` to open the map. Explored areas are revealed; unexplored zones remain grey.
- Click anywhere on the map to set a **waypoint** — follow the on‑screen marker.
- Each Signal Tower and landmark has a unique map icon.
- Open your Journal (`Tab` → Journal tab) to review discovered locations and collectibles.

> 🌅 **Don't rush the main quest.** Outbound's world is hand‑crafted and full of hidden surprises. Wander off the path — you'll be rewarded.
