---
title: How to Use Hiking Boots in Outbound
date: 2026-05-12
category: Guides
excerpt: Equip hiking boots to move faster, reduce stamina drain on rough terrain, and climb steep slopes more easily.
---

**Prerequisite:** You have unlocked and crafted the Hiking Boots.

1. Press `Tab` to open your inventory.
2. Click the **“Clothing”** tab at the top.
3. Locate the Hiking Boots in your backpack.
4. Drag them to the **Feet** slot on the right‑side equipment panel.
5. Release the mouse button – the boots are now equipped.
6. Effects (passive, no extra input needed):
   - 🏃 +15% movement speed
   - ⛰️ Reduced stamina drain on mountain/rough terrain
   - 🧗 Improved ability to climb steep slopes
