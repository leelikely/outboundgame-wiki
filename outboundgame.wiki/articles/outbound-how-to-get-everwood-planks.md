## 🔧 Method 1: Crafting (Recommended)

1. Collect **Everwood Logs** by chopping dead trees or picking them up near rivers.
2. Use the Sawmill in your van – place 2 Everwood Logs inside to craft 1 Everwood Plank.
3. This is the most stable and abundant method.

## 🏚️ Method 2: Scavenging

- Explore ruins, abandoned camps, and construction sites.
- Look for scattered plank piles on the ground.
- There’s a small chance to find ready‑made Everwood Planks.
- ⚠️ Very unreliable for bulk needs; only use as a last resort.
