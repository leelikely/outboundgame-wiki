# Outbound – Solo Scavenger or Multiplayer Shenanigans?

*Outbound* (also known as 拾光旅人) is a game of delightful contrasts. Play it alone, and you get a hardcore survival‑lite simulator. Bring friends, and it transforms into a laughter‑filled road trip.

---

## 🧑‍🌾 Single‑Player: A Meditative Survival Experience

When you play solo, the game takes on a more realistic tone. You wake up in the wilder­ness and must **gather resources, craft tools, and expand your van** all by yourself. Every log, every scrap of metal feels earned. It’s a quiet, methodical journey — perfect for players who enjoy peaceful isolation.

---

## 👫 Multiplayer: Pure Joy on Wheels

Switch to co‑op mode and everything changes. Up to four players can join the same world:

- 🚙 One person drives, while another cooks or builds.
- 🏕️ Set up a campsite together and decorate it with ridiculous furniture.
- 🤣 Goof around, race your vehicles, or simply chat while watching the sunset.

The game becomes a social sandbox where creativity and humor take over. There’s no wrong way to play — only good times.

---

## 🎯 Best of Both Worlds

Whether you want a solitary survival challenge or a shared laugh with friends, *Outbound* adapts beautifully. It’s a gem that caters to both introverts and extroverts alike.
