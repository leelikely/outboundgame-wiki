Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-10

Welcome to Outbound Guide (outboundgame.wiki). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require user registration and do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information. However, like most websites, certain non‑personal data may be automatically collected:

  • Log Data: Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may collect standard server logs including IP address, browser type, referring pages, and timestamps.
  • Cookies: We and our advertising partners (such as Google AdSense and Monetag) use cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized ads and analyze traffic.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

We display advertisements provided by third‑party networks, including but not limited to:

  • Google AdSense: Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to our site or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Monetag: Monetag is an advertising platform that may also use cookies and tracking technologies to deliver relevant advertisements. For more information, please refer to Monetag’s Privacy Policy.

These third‑party vendors may collect non‑personal information about your browsing activity to provide interest‑based advertising. You can generally opt out of many third‑party ad networks through the Digital Advertising Alliance or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use essential cookies for site functionality (e.g., remembering your preferences). Advertising partners also set cookies to measure ad performance and personalize content. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of the site may not function properly.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Aggregated, non‑personal data may be shared with analytics providers and advertising partners for the purposes described above.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
