About This Site

Welcome to Outbound Guide, a dedicated fan resource for the game Outbound (also known as 拾光旅人). We provide in‑depth guides, tips, and walkthroughs to help you get the most out of your camper van adventures.

Our Mission

We noticed there wasn’t a central, well‑organized English guide site for Outbound, so we built one. Our goal is to share knowledge, foster community discussion, and help both new and experienced players explore the open world with confidence.

Fan-Made & Unofficial

This website is a fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the developers or publishers of Outbound. All game assets, trademarks, and copyrights belong to their respective owners.

Content Updates

We update the site regularly as we discover new strategies and as the game evolves. If you have a tip or want to contribute a guide, feel free to reach out via our contact page.
